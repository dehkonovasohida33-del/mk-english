8. Our Family Traditions

Special activities that bring our family together.

🍚

Every Sunday

Every Sunday, we cook plov together. We enjoy eating and talking as a family.

🏕️

Once a Year

Once a year, we go to a dacha. We relax and enjoy spending time together outside the city.

🏡

Once a Month

Once a month, we visit our father's friend, Fayya. We spend time there and talk together.

🎂

Father's Birthday

We celebrate my father's birthday twice. Both celebrations are special for our family.

These traditions make our family closer. We enjoy cooking plov, visiting friends, going to the dacha and celebrating special days. Spending time together gives us happy memories.

9. Comparison and Conclusion

My father works outside the home, while my mother stays at home. My brother spends most of his time with our mother. I go to school in the afternoon and study at home.

We all have different daily routines and favourite activities. My father likes repairing things, my mother enjoys cooking, my brother likes running around the house, and I enjoy playing video games.

Our family traditions are also important to us. We cook plov every Sunday, go to a dacha once a year and visit my father's friend once a month. We also celebrate my father's birthday twice.

I love spending time with my family. These activities help us stay close, have fun and make happy memories together.
